## Notes

- T03 and T11 are offline cases of this matrix (no launch, scenario OFFLINE): T03 regenerates the 30 unseen demand episodes (6 test + 24 further) and compares the installed model with persistence per episode; T11 runs the planner against the independent oracle on 20 seeded edge states. T11 / T12 are also checked live in S6.
- Crossing criteria use the strict ground-truth measures of metrics_logger (no grace interval): contact, token overlap, robot / bus together with another robot / bus or a car in C, and entry without the grant or onto a pedestrian must be zero; a pedestrian stepping into a granted crossing must be answered by a measured standstill within 1000 ms.
- Every robot / bus + pedestrian episode in C is classified from the raw trace (crossing_occupancy.csv): a pedestrian intrusion when the pedestrian appeared in a crossing that the robot / bus already owned and occupied (the 80-85 s stimulus of S8, assignment 15.1: "existing movement stops safely as needed"), otherwise hub-caused. Hub-caused episodes must be zero; intrusions stay in the strict-occupancy count and are judged by the measured response.
- A REROUTE / NO_ROUTE must be followed by a measured standstill within 500 ms (declared budget; ground truth is sampled at 10 Hz).
